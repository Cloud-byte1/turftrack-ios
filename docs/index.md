# fairLie

fairLie is an iPhone golf-practice app for iOS 16 and later. It connects over Bluetooth Low Energy to a GolfMat pressure/FSR mat and, optionally, a second ESP32 with an Acconeer XM125 radar. The app turns practice strikes into feedback and saves sessions so golfers can review their progress.

The GolfMat is needed for live mat measurements. The optional radar adds a measured ball-speed reading. Without radar, ball speed is estimated. Carry distance, club speed, and attack angle are also estimates derived from the mat sensors.

You can continue without an account to use Practice, Progress, Home, and your profile. Guest profiles and sessions stay only on that iPhone. An account is required for the Clubhouse leaderboard, feed, challenges, and events.

The built-in swing simulator, examples, and test swings are clearly labeled **SIMULATED**. Sessions containing simulated swings remain on the iPhone and are not uploaded or ranked.
